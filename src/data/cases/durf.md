## Assignment

DURF 2030 wants to aid in the creation of 2030 cultural projects by being a platform that allows citizens, businesses ... to enable/fund local projects that have cultural significance.

The assignment (a bachelor's thesis) was to create a working platform prototype for DURF 2030 that encouraged citizen participation.

## Chalenge

Create a prototype that provides solutions for DURF 2030’s specific needs and where other current digital solutions or platforms failed at the time. The Prototype also needed to have a distinct ‘DURF 2030’ design and be based on and supported by lots of research.

## Approach

Because this was a group project, we started by dividing the amount of work amongst ourselves fairly equally.
Together with Gilles, I focussed on the front end while Miguel focussed on the back end (Firebase). Besides helping with the front end, I was also mainly responsible for the design, illustrations and research.

My biggest contributions to the front end were the homepage (with all its illustrations) and the “quiz” that gave users personalized tags based on their answers.
